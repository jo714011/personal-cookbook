## Instructions
1. Cut the potatoes into small pieces, smaller than you would for regular mashed potatoes.
2. Rinse the pieces and drain off the loose starch. This step is optional and makes little difference.
3. Put the potatoes in a pot and cover with water. Add the peeled garlic cloves if using.
4. Set the heat to low and hold the potatoes just below a simmer for about 20 minutes.
5. Raise the heat to a simmer and cook until the potatoes are soft enough to mash. For an even stronger effect, drain and fully cool the potatoes first, then simmer them until soft.
6. Drain the potatoes, saving a little of the cooking water.
7. Mash with a ricer for the finest texture, or whip with beaters.
8. Stir in the butter, green onions, salt, pepper, powdered milk, and a splash of the reserved cooking water.
9. Swirl a raw egg yolk into the hot potatoes at the table, if using.

## Notes
- The low-temperature hold is the key step. It changes the potato starch so the mash turns out smooth rather than gluey.
