## Instructions
1. Marinate the chicken with the ginger garlic paste, red chilli powder, and salt. Set aside for 15–20 minutes.
2. Heat some oil in a pan and fry the marinated chicken until cooked. Transfer it to a bowl.
3. In the same pan, cook the onions in a little oil and 1 tablespoon of the butter until softened.
4. Add the tomatoes and cashews.
5. Add a splash of water along with the garlic paste, salt, vinegar, sugar, garam masala, and Kashmiri chilli powder. Mix well and simmer for 15–20 minutes.
6. Blend the mixture into a fine purée.
7. Strain the purée back into the same pan, pressing well so as little as possible is wasted.
8. Stir in the remaining butter, the cream, the chicken, and the kasoori methi. Simmer for 5–7 minutes.
9. Garnish with a swirl of cream and a pinch of kasoori methi, and serve.
