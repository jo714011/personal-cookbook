## Instructions
### On its own
1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Toss the florets with the olive oil, salt, and pepper.
3. Spread them on a rimmed baking sheet with space between the pieces so they brown instead of steam.
4. Roast 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway, until the stems are fork-tender and the edges are browned and crisp.
5. Finish with a squeeze of lemon or some Parmesan, if you like.

### Alongside the Classic Glazed Meatloaf
1. While the meatloaf bakes unglazed at 375°F for its first 40 minutes, prep and season the broccoli on its own sheet pan.
2. When you glaze the meatloaf, put the broccoli on the upper rack. Both bake at 375°F for the last 15–20 minutes.
3. When the meatloaf comes out to rest, raise the oven to 425°F and roast the broccoli 5–10 more minutes, until the edges char.
4. Serve when the meatloaf's 10-minute rest is up.

## Notes
- Smaller florets can be done in 15–18 minutes at 425°F, so start checking early.
- Keep the broccoli off the meatloaf pan: the fat that drips off the loaf steams the broccoli instead of letting it brown.
