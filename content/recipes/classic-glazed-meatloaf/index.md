## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 375°F and line a rimmed baking sheet with foil or parchment.
2. In a large bowl, stir the panko into the milk and let it soak until soft, about 5 minutes.
3. Add the eggs, onion, garlic, Worcestershire, parsley, salt, pepper, and paprika to the panade and stir to combine.
4. Add the ground beef and mix gently with your hands just until everything is evenly combined. Overmixing makes the loaf dense.
5. Shape into a loaf about 9 x 5 inches on the lined baking sheet.
6. In a small bowl, whisk together the ketchup, brown sugar, and vinegar.
7. Bake the loaf without the glaze for 40 minutes.
8. Spread the glaze over the top and bake 15–20 minutes more, until the center reads 160°F on an instant-read thermometer.
9. Let rest 10 minutes before slicing so the juices set.

## Notes
- Baking unglazed first keeps the glaze from burning and helps it stick.
- Prep and total times are (estimated).
- Serve with mashed potatoes and green beans.

## Variations
- For an extra-juicy loaf, swap ½ lb of the beef for ground pork or Italian sausage.
- In a loaf pan, add 10–15 minutes and pour off the fat before glazing.
