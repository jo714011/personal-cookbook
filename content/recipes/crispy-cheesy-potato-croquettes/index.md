## Instructions
1. Peel the potatoes, cut them into large chunks, and boil in salted water until completely tender.
2. Drain well and mash until smooth and dry. Don't add milk or butter.
3. Let the potatoes cool slightly, then mix in the cornstarch, garlic powder, paprika, pepper, salt, and parsley until you have a soft, pliable mixture.
4. Flatten a clementine-sized portion in your hand, set a cube of mozzarella in the center, and seal the potato around the cheese. Shape as you like.
5. Optional: freeze the croquettes for 15–20 minutes so they hold their shape while frying.
6. Heat oil in a skillet, enough to come at least halfway up the croquettes.
7. Fry over medium heat for 2–3 minutes per side, until evenly golden.
8. Drain on a paper towel-lined plate and serve hot.

## Notes
- Mash the potatoes dry, with no milk or butter, or the mixture will be too wet to shape.
