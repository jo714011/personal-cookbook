## Instructions

### Mashed Potatoes
1. Place the potatoes in a saucepan, cover with cold salted water, and bring to a boil. Cook until fork-tender, 15–20 minutes.
2. Drain and mash with the butter and milk until smooth. Season with salt and pepper, then cover to keep warm.

### Beef Medallions
3. Pat the beef dry and season with salt and pepper. Wrap a slice of bacon around the side of each medallion and secure with a toothpick.
4. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the medallions 3–4 minutes per side, until the bacon is crisp and the beef reaches your desired doneness. Transfer to a plate and tent with foil.
5. Reduce the heat to medium and add the butter and garlic to the skillet. Cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add the beef broth and balsamic vinegar, scraping up any browned bits, and simmer until slightly thickened, 2–3 minutes.

### Sautéed Squash
6. While the beef rests, heat the olive oil in a separate skillet over medium-high heat. Add the zucchini and yellow squash and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender and lightly browned, 6–8 minutes.
7. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute more. Season with salt.

### To Serve
8. Remove the toothpicks from the medallions. Plate the mashed potatoes, medallions, and squash. Spoon the pan sauce over the beef and garnish with chives.
