## Instructions
1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into 1–2 inch chunks. Put them in a large saucepan, cover with an inch or two of water, and add 2 teaspoons of salt.
2. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Cook 10–20 minutes, until the potatoes are very tender and just starting to fall apart.
3. While the potatoes cook, blend the garlic, parsley, chives, tarragon, crème fraîche, and milk on medium-high until very smooth, about 1 minute.
4. Drain the potatoes and return them to the warm pot. Add the butter and mash until fairly smooth, or push them through a ricer for the smoothest result. Mash gently and stop right away if they start to feel sticky or gluey.
5. Pour in the herb puree and stir gently to combine. Add more milk if needed; the potatoes firm up as they sit, so you may want to add more after a little while.
6. Taste and add more salt and pepper as you like. If the potatoes are still lumpy, press them through a colander with the back of a ladle into a large bowl.
7. Serve right away, topped with a drizzle of melted butter, a sprinkle of chives, and pepper.

## Notes
- The potatoes stiffen as they sit, so loosen with a little extra milk if they wait before serving.
