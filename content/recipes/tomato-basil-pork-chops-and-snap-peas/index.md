## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2. Season the pork chops all over with 1½ teaspoons of the salt and ¾ teaspoon of the pepper. Set them on one side of a 17x12-inch rimmed baking sheet.
3. Bake for 15 minutes.
4. Brush the balsamic glaze onto the chops, then top with the mozzarella and arugula. Raise the oven temperature to broil.
5. In a large bowl, toss the snap peas with 1 tablespoon of the oil, ½ teaspoon of the salt, and ¼ teaspoon of the pepper. Spread them on the empty side of the pan.
6. Broil for 5 minutes.
7. In a small bowl, stir together the tomatoes, basil, the remaining 2 tablespoons oil, and the remaining ¼ teaspoon each of salt and pepper. Spoon over the pork, then garnish with more basil and balsamic glaze if you like.
