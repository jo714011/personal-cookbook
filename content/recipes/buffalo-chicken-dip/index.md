## Instructions
1. Heat the chicken and hot pepper sauce in a skillet over medium heat until warmed through, 3–5 minutes.
2. Stir in the cream cheese, sour cream, and ranch seasoning. Cook, stirring, until smooth and heated through, 3–5 minutes more.
3. Mix in half of the Cheddar, then transfer the mixture to a slow cooker. Sprinkle the remaining Cheddar over the top.
4. Cover and cook on low until hot and bubbly, about 35 minutes.
5. Serve warm with celery sticks and crackers for dipping.

## Notes
- The original recipe notes you can substitute about 3 cups of cooked, shredded chicken breast for the canned chicken. Finely chop or shred it for a smoother dip.
- Swapped the ranch dressing for 1 cup low-fat sour cream plus 1 tablespoon ranch seasoning, per a reader tip — great recipe, this tweak works well.
