## Instructions
1. Put the potatoes in the stockpot, cover with cold water by an inch, and salt the water well. Bring to a boil and simmer until fork-tender, 15–18 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, brown the ground beef in a skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it into small crumbles, 7–8 minutes.
3. Drain the potatoes and return them to the stockpot over low heat.
4. Add the browned beef (drippings and all), the butter, and the warm milk to the stockpot.
5. Mash everything together until the potatoes are mostly smooth and the beef is mixed throughout.
6. Season with the salt and pepper, taste, and adjust. Serve hot.

## Notes
- Recreated from memory of Mom making it — quantities and times are estimates (estimated). Adjust after the first cook.
- Keeping the beef drippings in is what makes the potatoes taste beefy.

## Variations
- Top with brown gravy, or stir in a handful of shredded cheddar.
